about kota doriya work

Kota Doriya saree, also known as Kota Doria, is a traditional handwoven saree that originates from Kota, a city in Rajasthan. It’s known for its lightweight texture, checkered patterns, and airy feel, making it perfect for hot and humid weather.
1. Fabric & Weaving:
Kota Doriya is made from a blend of cotton and silk yarns. The unique check pattern, known as “khat,” is created using a square-weaving technique on traditional pit looms.
2. Appearance:
The saree has a transparent and glossy texture with fine square motifs. It’s soft yet crisp, giving an elegant drape.
3. Designs:
You’ll find both plain and embellished versions. Many are decorated with zari borders, embroidery, block prints, or hand-painted motifs, depending on whether it’s casual or festive wear. 4. Comfort:
These sarees are very breathable, light, and easy to manage—ideal for summer and daily wear.
5. Varieties:
There are pure cotton, silk-cotton blends, and zari Kota sarees. The silk adds shine, while cotton keeps it cool and comfortable.
6. Care:
Hand wash in cold water or dry clean for silk blends to maintain the saree’s texture and sheen
