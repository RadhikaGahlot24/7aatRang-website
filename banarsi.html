about banarsi work

A Banarasi saree is one of the most famous and luxurious types of sarees from India. It comes from Varanasi (Banaras) in Uttar Pradesh, a city known for its rich weaving tradition that dates back hundreds of years.
1. Fabric and Weaving
Banarasi sarees are usually made of pure silk, though there are also variants in cotton, organza, and georgette. The weaving is done on handlooms with intricate designs created using zari (threads of gold or silver).
2. Design and Motifs
The designs are heavily inspired by Mughal art — you’ll see motifs like flowers, leaves, vines (known as “bel”), peacocks, and paisleys. Some sarees also have small figures or scenes woven into the pallu or border.
3. Types of Banarasi Sarees
Katan Banarasi: Pure silk with a rich, smooth texture. Organza (Kora) Banarasi: Light and sheer fabric with zari and silk embroidery. Georgette Banarasi: Soft and flowy, suitable for light occasions. Tissue Banarasi: Shimmery fabric with golden or silver sheen, often worn for weddings.
